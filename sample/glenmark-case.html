A saved boxed-warning wording difference

Lamictal’s label adds HLA-B*1502 to its serious-rash risk factors. A later published Glenmark chewable-tablet label retains the earlier risk language. This is one example for a labeling expert to review.

The publication sequence

May 7, 2025Lamictal v43 · previous wording
November 17, 2025Lamictal v44 · changed boxed warning
February 2, 2026Glenmark v26 · earlier wording retained

These are DailyMed publication dates. Lamictal v44’s SPL effective date is October 10, 2025; its Recent Major Changes box lists October 2025. Glenmark v26’s effective date is January 29, 2026. Lamictal v45, published August 20, 2026, retains the same boxed warning.

The text to check

Brand v44: the revised risk-factor sentence includes:

or (4) the presence of the HLA-B*1502 allele.

Glenmark v26: the saved boxed warning retains these excerpts:

Other than age, there are as yet no factors identified …
There are suggestions, yet to be proven …

The HLA-B*1502 term is absent from this saved boxed-warning section. Open Glenmark v26.

The observation concerns retained DailyMed wording. It does not establish a missed deadline, regulatory noncompliance, submission status or current distribution.
